# Production business outcomes

These observations belong to the production experience represented by this public implementation. They are not synthetic benchmark results.

- Approximately six weeks after launch, requests handled outside manager working hours increased by 15%.
- Requests closed without manager participation increased from approximately 5% to approximately 36%.
- The company reported an increase in completed sales, but no percentage was disclosed.

The figures are reported as observed after six weeks. They are not a causal experiment, a guarantee, or a result of the synthetic demo. The observed mechanism was that customers received an immediate initial consultation and could contact a manager for order-specific details instead of continuing their search elsewhere.
